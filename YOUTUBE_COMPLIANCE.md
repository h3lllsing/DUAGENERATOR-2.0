# YouTube Compliance Checklist

Ensuring Dua Video Generator complies with YouTube Terms of Service.

## Content Requirements

### ✅ Allowed
- Original Islamic dua content
- Proper attribution to sources
- Family-friendly material
- Educational/spiritual purpose
- No misleading thumbnails

### ❌ Prohibited
- Copyrighted music without license
- Misleading content or metadata
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Violent or graphic content
- Spam or deceptive practices

## Metadata Compliance

### Title
- Accurate representation of content
- No misleading keywords
- Appropriate length (under 100 chars)
- No excessive capitalization

### Description
- Honest description of video content
- Source attribution for duas
- No deceptive links
- Appropriate tags

### Tags
- Relevant to content only
- No misleading tags
- Mix of specific and broad terms
- Include Arabic/Urdu terms

## Audio Compliance

### Music/Sounds
- No copyrighted background music
- Use royalty-free ambient sounds
- Proper volume levels
- Clear speech audio

### Voice
- Appropriate voice quality
- No voice manipulation for deception
- Clear pronunciation
- Proper pacing

## Visual Compliance

### Thumbnails
- Accurate representation
- No misleading imagery
- Appropriate resolution
- No clickbait elements

### Video Content
- Appropriate resolution (1080p)
- Stable footage
- No excessive flashing
- Clean transitions

## Upload Best Practices

### Scheduling
- Consistent upload schedule
- Avoid spam uploads
- Respect upload limits
- Consider audience time zones

### Engagement
- Respond to comments appropriately
- No artificial engagement
- No sub4sub practices
- Genuine audience building

## Monetization Rules

### Eligibility
- Follow YouTube Partner Program policies
- No misleading monetization
- Appropriate ad placement
- No deceptive click practices

### Revenue
- Honest revenue reporting
- No artificial inflation
- Comply with tax requirements
- Respect payment terms

## Violations

### If Content Removed
1. Review YouTube's notification
2. Understand the violation
3. Fix the issue
4. Appeal if appropriate
5. Document the incident

### Account Strikes
1. Take immediate action
2. Review all content
3. Remove violating material
4. Implement prevention measures
5. Monitor account status

## Reporting

### Internal Tracking
- Log all uploads in `data/upload_log.txt`
- Track video performance
- Monitor comment sentiment
- Record any issues

### Incident Response
1. Identify the issue
2. Assess severity
3. Take corrective action
4. Document lessons learned
5. Update policies if needed

## Regular Audits

- Monthly content review
- Quarterly compliance check
- Annual policy update
- Continuous monitoring
