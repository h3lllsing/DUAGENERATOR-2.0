1. Ye page kya hai?

Ye ghar ka video studio hai — 76 dua videos yahan ready hain. Upar right corner ka gol chakra batata hai kitni % videos mukammal hain.

2. Video dekhna

Card ki tasveer pe click karo ya neeche PLAY dabao — video player khul jayegi.

3. Dua dhundna

Upar search box me dua ka naam ya reference likho, ya category chips (morning, salah...) se chhaanto.

4. Nayi videos banana

⚡ RENDER ALL ek dafa dabao — saari videos ek-ek karke banengi (har video ko taqreeban 2 minute lagte hain). Upar progress bar chalegi. Browser band kar do to bhi kaam hota rahega. ⏹ CANCEL sirf zaroorat pe dabana.

5. YouTube upload (ahtiyat!)

▶️ YOUTUBE dabao, panel khul jayega:
• Pehle login dekho: sabz dot + "logged in" hona chahiye. Nahi hai to ADVANCED kholo → 🔒 Authenticate Channel 1
• Channel select karo (Channel 1)
• Sab se pehle DRY-RUN (test) chalao — is me YouTube pe kuch upload NAHI hota, sirf test hai
• Theek ho to LIVE UPLOAD select karke 1 se 6 duas pe tick lagao aur neeche wala bada START button dabao
• Privacy unlisted hi rehne do

6. Settings ⚙️

Channel name, @handle aur Video Style Preset isi me hai. Style abhi Auto hai (har video ka apna look). Save ke baad NAYE renders naya style lenge — purani videos pe koi asar nahi.

7. Ye cheezein MAT chhedo

• 🗑️ DELETE — dua hamesha ke liye mit jayegi!
• ✍️ EDIT — text badalne se video kharab ho sakti hai (ye sirf un-rendered cards pe aata hai)
• force re-TTS ka tick — awaz dobara ban jayegi
• ADVANCED/CREDENTIALS wala hissa technical hai

8. Folders

📁 VIDEOS / AUDIO buttons se files seedha khulti hain. 🕑 HISTORY me pichhle renders ka record milta hai.
