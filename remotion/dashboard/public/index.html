📖 Kaise Use Karein / کیسے استعمال کریں

1. Ye page kya hai?

Ye ghar ka video studio hai. Upar right corner ka gol chakra batata hai kitni % videos mukammal hain.

2. Video dekhna

Card ki tasveer pe click karo ya PLAY dabao — video player khul jayegi.

3. Dua dhundna

Upar search box me dua ka naam ya reference likho, ya category chips se chhaanto.

4. Videos banana

Har card pe Render button hai — sirf us dua ki video banegi. Jo bani hain wo automatically skip ho jayengi.

5. YouTube upload

▶️ YOUTUBE dabao → channel chuno → cards se 1-6 duas chuno → UPLOAD SELECTED dabao. Privacy unlisted hi rehne do.

6. Uploaded videos

✓ Uploaded button pe click karo — YouTube pe jo videos hain wo dikhengi. Unka link copy kar sakte ho ya Re-upload kar sakte ho (different channel ke liye).

7. Settings ⚙️

Channel name, @handle, Video Style, YouTube Auth sab isi me hai.

8. Ye cheezein MAT chhedo

• 🗑️ DELETE — dua hamesha ke liye mit jayegi!
• ✍️ EDIT — sirf un-rendered cards pe aata hai
